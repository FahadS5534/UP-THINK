Why does an n × n real symmetric matrix always have n real eigenvalues and an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors?

λ Companion AI 10:43 AM

The foundation of the Spectral Theorem for real symmetric matrices rests on two key mathematical guarantees:

Formal Identity A = Aᵀ ∈ ℝⁿˣⁿ
Av = λv &implies v*Av = v*(λv) = λ||v||²
Taking conjugate transpose: (Av)*v = v*A*v = v*Aᵀv = λ̄||v||²

Since λ||v||² = λ̄||v||² and ||v|| > 0, we conclude λ = λ̄. Hence, all eigenvalues are strictly real.

Key Takeaways
check_circle Orthogonality of Distinct Eigenspaces: If λ₁ ≠ λ₂, then v₁ᵀv₂ = 0 automatically.
check_circle Complete Basis Guaranteed: Repeated eigenvalues still possess geometric multiplicity equal to algebraic multiplicity.
