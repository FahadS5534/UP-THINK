## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, high-precision academic analytics aesthetic designed for higher education educators, researchers, and students. It blends the intellectual authority of archival publications with the technical sharpness of modern computational tooling.

The interface prioritizes focus, calm analytical rigor, and clarity through restrained dark glassmorphism. Visual depth is achieved not through bright neon accents or generic corporate gradients, but via warm charcoal layers, delicate frosted boundaries, and a singular, electric acid-lime focal point that directs attention to critical interventions and academic mastery.

- **Tone & Mood:** Cerebral, focused, authoritative, discreetly progressive.
- **Visual Expression:** Dark editorial glassmorphism, organic charcoal undertones, structural typography, razor-thin translucent borders.

## Colors

The palette relies on a base of warm, earthen near-black substrates, layering information through calibrated luminance rather than saturation.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#10110F` — Deep earthen charcoal, foundational canvas.
- **Surface Level 1 (Mid Ground):** `#171916` — Component containers and structural cards.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Glass):** `#20231F` — Interactive panels, popovers, and elevated modal panels with translucent backdrop-filter blurs.
- **Border / Perimeter:** `rgba(235, 239, 221, 0.10)` — Micro-line separation across all panels to simulate cut glass edges.

### Accents & Indicators
- **Primary / Mastery:** `#C8F169` (Acid Lime) — Reserved exclusively for primary actions, current mastery states, and high-impact interventions.
- **Secondary:** `#D5A879` (Muted Amber) — Academic citations, secondary analytical tags, and temporal markers.
- **High-Risk Alert:** `#E98278` — Critical performance drop, course attrition probability.
- **Medium-Risk Alert:** `#E6BC72` — Stalled engagement, partial comprehension gaps.
- **Low-Risk / Stable:** `#A9C98B` — Steady progression, low risk.

### Typography & Content Shades
- **Text High Contrast:** `#F0F0E7` — Primary headers, numerical metrics, active labels.
- **Text Medium Contrast:** `#A2A69A` — Body text, descriptive summaries.
- **Text Low Contrast:** `#70766B` — Secondary metadata, disabled states, grid rules.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere: analytical, rigorous, and highly legible.

- **Display & Headings:** Set in `Space Grotesk` with slightly condensed tracking to invoke technical field journals and scientific data printouts. Metric numerals leverage tabular lining figures when visualizing comparative scorecards.
- **Body & Longform:** Set in `DM Sans` for high sustained reading comfort. It softens the mechanical precision of headings with humanist geometric terminals.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered in `Space Grotesk` uppercase or tracked medium weights, conveying structured taxonomy and machine-readable data tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid system across desktop views, shifting to a 6-column structure on tablet, and a 4-column compact layout on mobile.

- **Rhythm:** An 8-point base spatial cadence organizes both dense metric dashboards and long-form narrative feedback. 
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop experiences utilize generous 40px (`2.5rem`) canvas margins with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters. Mobile configurations tighten to 16px (`1rem`) outer boundaries to preserve screen real estate for dense student rosters and course graphs.
- **Reflow Rules:** Analytical charts and longitudinal progress graphs maintain strict aspect ratios; on mobile viewports, dual-metric columns collapse to vertically stacked cards with horizontal scroll triggers for large data tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted planar layering and refined specular rim highlights instead of pronounced drop shadows.

- **Backdrop Blurs:** Elevated containers utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` paired with 85% opacity `#20231F`.
- **Specular Glass Borders:** Every elevated panel uses a 1px solid stroke with `rgba(235, 239, 221, 0.10)`. When hovered, interactive panels highlight their perimeter to `rgba(200, 241, 105, 0.35)`.
- **Ambient Lighting:** Shadows are extremely restrained: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.50)` for floating contextual drawers and navigation trays. No saturated color drop-shadows or fuzzy neon glows are permitted.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, architectural corner geometry (Level 1 - Soft: 4px base radius, 8px on cards, 12px on structural viewports).

This subtle rounding avoids the playful softness of consumer apps while preventing the abrasive harshness of zero-radius brutalism. Metric badges, data chips, and status nodes retain structured 4px corners, whereas modal dialogs and core container cards scale up to 8px.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#C8F169` background with `#10110F` bold `Space Grotesk` text. Flat finish, zero drop shadow. On hover, background shifts to a brighter lime tint (`#D5F788`).
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, `#20231F` background at 60% opacity, 1px perimeter of `rgba(235, 239, 221, 0.15)`, and `#F0F0E7` text.
- **Destructive/Risk Action:** Subtle tinted background (`rgba(233, 130, 120, 0.12)`) with `#E98278` text and 1px border (`rgba(233, 130, 120, 0.25)`).

### Input Fields & Selects
- Set within `#171916` background containers with a default border of `rgba(235, 239, 221, 0.10)`.
- Input text uses `#F0F0E7`, placeholder text sits at `#70766B`.
- Active focus state introduces a crisp 1px stroke of `#C8F169` without outward drop shadows.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Composed of `#171916` bases, transitioning to `#20231F` for elevated or floating states.
- Bound by a continuous 1px outline of `rgba(235, 239, 221, 0.10)`.
- Padding maintains an internal rhythm of `space-lg` (24px) for dashboard analytical cards.

### Risk & Mastery Indicators (Chips / Badges)
- **High Risk:** Background `rgba(233, 130, 120, 0.10)`, text `#E98278`, border `rgba(233, 130, 120, 0.20)`.
- **Medium Risk:** Background `rgba(230, 188, 114, 0.10)`, text `#E6BC72`, border `rgba(230, 188, 114, 0.20)`.
- **Low Risk / Mastery:** Background `rgba(169, 201, 139, 0.10)`, text `#A9C98B`, border `rgba(169, 201, 139, 0.20)`.
- Rendered with compact padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal) in uppercase `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox squares utilize an 8px radius box with 1px `rgba(235, 239, 221, 0.20)` border.
- Selected state fills with `#C8F169`, displaying an obsidian check mark icon (`#10110F`).

### Specialized Component: Longitudinal Knowledge Cohort Matrix
- An interactive academic matrix grid displaying individual learning outcomes. 
- Individual student nodes sit within a dark frosted grid; hover reveals mastery velocity, retention degradation rate, and flagged intervention paths framed in editorial glass panels.
